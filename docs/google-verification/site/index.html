BooxDay

A free, open-source daily planner for Onyx BOOX e-ink tablets: a calendar, handwritten memos and handwritten task lists on one screen, made for the pen.

What it does

  • Month calendar with each day's events and due tasks.
  • Handwritten memo pages for every day, and handwritten task lists with checkboxes, due dates, reminders and repeating tasks.
  • Sync with Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar, or keep a calendar only on the tablet.
  • Optional Task Sync with Google Tasks, so your tasks are on your phone too.
  • Optional backup of your planner to your own Google Drive.
  • BOOX Notes integration: see your BOOX notes on the days you wrote them and link them to events.
  • Search across memos, tasks and notes.
  • Optional AI features on your own OpenRouter account. BooxDay has no AI key of its own.

Your Google account

Connecting a Google account is optional. BooxDay works without one. If you connect one, BooxDay asks Google for:

  • Google Calendar: to show your calendars and events on the tablet, and to add, change or delete events when you do that in BooxDay.
  • Google Drive, only files BooxDay creates: to save your backups in a BooxDay folder in your Drive and restore them, if you turn on Cloud Backup. BooxDay can't see your other Drive files.
  • Google Tasks, only if you turn on Task Sync: to show the tasks of the lists you pick on the tablet, and to save the tasks you add, tick or delete in BooxDay to them.
  • Your email address: to show which account is connected.

BooxDay has no server. Your calendar and task data goes straight between your tablet and Google, and is kept on your tablet. The developer never receives it. The Privacy Policy explains the details, including when AI features you choose to use send data to OpenRouter.

BooxDay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Install

BooxDay isn't on Google Play. Download booxday-x.y.z.apk from the latest release on the tablet and open it. The install guide walks through every step. BooxDay then updates itself from the same page.
